Your eCard Setup Guide

Follow these simple steps to open and set up your eCard

Download again

Step 1

Locate the Downloaded File

Check your browser’s download folder or the downloads bar at the top of your browser window.

Step 2

Double-click to Start Installation

Double-click the file to launch the installer. If prompted with a security warning, click “Yes” or “Run”.

Step 3

Follow the Setup Wizard

Follow the instructions in the Setup Wizard. Accept the terms and click Next to complete installation.
